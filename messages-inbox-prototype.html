How it works (annotations)

  1. 124-hour window is enforced in the UI. Free-text is only allowed within 24h of the patient's last message. A live countdown shows in the header; when it expires the composer swaps to a template picker (see Fatima Begum). This is a WhatsApp rule — break it and sends silently fail.
  2. 2Claim / assign. One staffer claims a conversation so two people don't reply to the same patient. "Mine" filter = what you've claimed. Flows into the audit log.
  3. 3Auto-stitched context. Each inbound is linked to the last report/bill sent to that number, so staff instantly see why the patient is writing.
  4. 4Patient auto-link + branch scope. Inbound phone → patient record (via visit phone). Unmatched numbers show "No patient match". Conversations are branch-scoped like every other worklist.

Mobile view

The 3-pane inbox collapses to a single pane — list, then thread. The patient-context rail becomes a tappable strip. Same navy chrome + Sheet nav your staff already use on phones.
