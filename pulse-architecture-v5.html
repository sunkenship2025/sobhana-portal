Pulse — architecture v5

Everything below is stated because it was measured, not because it sounded right. Where a claim has a number, the number is from an instrument in the repo that can be re-run.

deterministic model

The laws, and what each one cost to learn

1 · An invariant in a prompt is not an invariant.

Every rule that stayed in a prompt failed again. Every one moved into code held. "A rate is not a total" was reworded three times and ignored three times; it held the first run after becoming a repair guard.

dues definition · paise convention · rate-vs-total · test grain · commission basis

2 · The model proposes. Code decides.

Not "the model reasons and code checks" — code also does the grounding, the stopping and the enforcement. An empty next is "I am done" wearing a structured coat, so the loop stops on convergence it can measure, never on the model's appetite.

3 · A guard that can refuse correct work is worse than no guard.

Learned four separate times in one day: a time filter that turned a correct all-time figure into month-to-date; a JGG regex that confirmed a fix that had not worked; a validator rejecting SQL it merely failed to parse; a registry guard that answered "no patients have dues" against a true 11.

every gate now falls back permissively, and says so in its own comment

4 · Identity travels with the number.

A figure without its period, scope and dimension is indistinguishable from one over a different window. The investigator was sent {step, label, tool, result} and compared a 30-day figure against a 90-day one, called the 6.6× ratio a contradiction, and abandoned a CT ROI question that was three numbers and one division.

5 · Absence of evidence in one index is not absence in the business.

An index miss, a policy block and a failed lookup are all facts about us. Reported as facts about the centre they are worse than silence — the owner named three real enum values and was told the system had no such thing.

6 · Measure the baseline before building the mechanism.

Three subsystems were correctly not built this way, and two estimates of mine shrank to nothing under measurement.

learned catalogue · calculation engine · job-classification rebuild

7 · The instruments are wrong about as often as the product.

Eight instrument corrections in one session — a probe sharing the code's blind spot, a 130-char truncation that invented a defect, a stopwatch measuring the knowledge build, an assertion encoding one implementation of a right answer. Every measurement here has been re-derived at least once.

The pipeline

1 · Entry & authorizationindex.ts · routes/pulse.ts

Small talk, follow-up detection, entity picker, entity card. Authorization is settled at the door — maySeePeople(req) → rowLevel — and travels as policy. It is never re-litigated by a flag the planner sets from the wording of a question.

↓
2 · Knowledgeknowledge.ts · built once, ~80s, 819 concepts

Live introspection: schema graph, every Postgres enum, branches, departments, test codes and names, payout categories. Concepts carry a family — a test's category, a category's service kind — so terms can disambiguate each other.

↓
3 · ResolutionscoreMatch · resolveRanked · termsIn

Ranked and deterministic: exact 1.00 · normalized 0.95 · prefix 0.75r · partial 0.75r · word 0.70r · substring 0.40r, where r is the length ratio. Per question, not per term — "ct scan machine" resolves scan → IMAGING, and only then is it decidable that ct means the CT/MRI category and not Clotting Time.

↓
4 · PlanaskPlan — given the resolved terms

Goal, analytical job, spec, first steps. The only inferred thing in the resolution path is which words become a scope term.

↓
5 · Spec grounding & bindingcompleteSpec · compileBindings · BindingAuthority

Terms re-resolved against live data; the planner's guess is overwritten when resolution succeeds and dropped when the value does not exist. Authority decides force: a period the owner named binds; a planner default is carried but never spoken, because a binding stated in the context is an instruction whatever hedge sits beside it.

↓
6 · Tools — 17registry 8 · operational 7 · semantic 1 · generated 1

Registry and operational tools have never failed in production. The generated query path carries every guard: spec verification, rate-vs-total, test grain, commission basis, due definition, money alias.

↓
7 · Verificationvalidator.ts · sqlscope.ts (AST)

Safe? no writes, no secrets, no cartesian products, no fan-out. Would it return a person? projected columns, only when policy forbids. Does the predicate restrict the ANSWER? walks outward from the result statement through referenced CTEs — and not through outer joins, which add columns and remove no rows. Did the committed scope survive? including into registry steps, which had no such check at all.

↓
8 · Investigationrun.ts · investigation.ts

Hypotheses with requirements; enforce() reopens a claim settled without the analysis it named. Stops on resolved, or measured stagnation — three rounds earning nothing, belief unchanged despite fresh rows, or nothing satisfying a requirement. Ceilings are an emergency brake and hitting one marks the answer incomplete. Comparability is checked before a contradiction may mean anything, in three states: comparable survives, different is dropped, unknown becomes an open question.

↓
9 · Job → contract → capabilitycontract.ts · capability.ts

Thirteen jobs, declared not matched. requires is a hard structural gate, buildable asks whether the renderer could construct its input. continuous now separates a measurement from a per-category count, so a histogram can no longer claim a breakdown. No question text reaches this layer.

↓
10 · Write-upaskResponse — retry-guarded

Segments, artifact choices, sized opportunities. A truncated response salvages its parseable prefix — never by closing an unterminated string, because a half-written WHERE would close into SQL that runs and returns a confidently wrong number.

↓
11 · Derivation & post-checksderiveView.ts · grounding.ts · opportunity.ts

One shared derivation feeds every renderer: shares, totals, remainder, concentration — arithmetic, never authored by the model. A field that cannot justify itself is absent, not null. Grounding admits exact, derived, or ordinary; anything else is sent back. Opportunities rank rupees × confidence × causality; unsized candidates are considered, never recommended.

Open, honestly

  • TEST_BILLED is flaky. ₹1,03,400 on most runs, a period-scoped figure on others — and the period it then names is one the SQL does not contain. A period claimed in prose and absent from every step is now checkable and not yet checked.
  • Stagnation has fired once in production, on a genuinely stuck run. Still the least-exercised mechanism.
  • recheckScope has never fired. Insurance against a bug the ranked resolver mostly prevents.
  • No feedback signal. Every quality judgment this session came from reading logs by hand. It does not scale past one person with a long afternoon.
  • ct remains genuinely ambiguous — a live test code and an imaging abbreviation. Family disambiguation settles it inside a question that names a family and nowhere else.
