One collision explains most of what was wrong, and one number is still missing.

The collision

do u think the 50 lakhs on ct scan machine will be worth it

CT is a live test code. It means CLOTTING TIME — a blood test — and the resolver matched it exactly, at confidence 1.00. A question about a fifty lakh scanner was being priced against a coagulation assay. Every CT figure came back 0, and "I could not establish it" was the system reporting its own name collision as a fact about your business.

The family mechanism built for exactly this failed three times over: scanner scored 0.43 against the concept scan because a length ratio treats an English suffix as evidence against a match; the family link is a chain (test → payout category → service kind) and only one level was held, so no test could ever match IMAGING; and the bare resolver appeared in three places while only one knew about families.

CT bare           CLOTTING TIME, CT-BRAIN PLAIN
CT in scanner Q   CT-BRAIN PLAIN, CT TEMPORAL BONES, CT LUMBAR SPINE
CT in lab Q       CLOTTING TIME          <- still right

The payback question answers now — 76 months — and the same way every time.

The same lesson, in different clothes

  • A period is a property of the query that ran, not the plan before it. Falling back to the plan labelled a correct all-time ₹1,03,400 as "for the period 1–13 September 2026".
  • You name a value; the column is our inference — and ours was wrong. Enforcing it refused every correct route: 19 of 24 steps failed.
  • Arithmetic had nowhere to happen. The writer is rightly forbidden to compute, and derive only divides two named metrics.
  • Cash belongs to payments, not orders. Commission on orders billed in a window ÷ cash collected in it is two different populations.
  • A constraint too weak to filter on is too weak to label with. "CT run-rate of ₹18,93,725" was centre-wide revenue — a capital decision 29× too optimistic.
  • A rule enforced on one path is one the next caller walks around.
  • "Nobody lapsed" and "I have no earlier period" are not the same claim. Your order history starts 1 July 2026, so a 180-day look-back lands entirely before any data exists — and a loss that cannot exist was asserted as real.

The ratio worth keeping

Eleven product bugs found after the model account ran dry, against roughly twenty defects in my own instruments and fixtures — a grader matching scan counts against rupees, a harness scoring billing failures as an 8/29 quality score, a replay pairing steps by an index that did not align, a mock mixing paise with rupees, a redaction turning the branches CNT and BLN into people.

Four of those I caught before filing a fix for something that worked. The honest summary of the week is that the architecture was in better shape than the things measuring it, and the measuring is what got rebuilt.
