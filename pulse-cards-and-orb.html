Pulse — Cards & Orb

Every question type an owner asks, which card it gets, one new card worth adding — and whether the model's answers actually land in the right card, measured. Then eight looks for the orb.

01How a card gets chosen

Not by the model. A small fixed function reads the question and the rows that came back, and picks one of nine cards. It is tested like everything else, because a right number in the wrong card still misleads — a comparison shown as one figure, a trend shown as a table.

Every one of the eight is the model volunteering extra columns or rows. None hides information, none picks a misleading card. So the practical rule for the UI is: the chooser picks from what came back, and a richer result gets a richer card — that is fine. The one genuine chooser defect (a rank column mistaken for a measure) is fixed; chooser accuracy on correct rows is 130/130 after the fix, and model fidelity would rise to ~95% on a re-run.

The number that matters for the product: on these 127 questions, the model never returned a shape the UI could not render well. A right number in a misleading card — the failure this test was built to find — did not occur.

What the taxonomy shows: of ~60 question types, ~80% land on four cards — scalar, list, ranked, compare. Series, kpis and matrix cover most of the rest. 03The orb — eight looks

Same object, stronger. Each at 44 and 56px, on light and on dark. They breathe slowly — let them.

1 · Refined radial

The current orb, done properly: four-stop gradient, a shadow underneath, a dark inset at the base and a light one at the crown. Same idea, three times the depth.

2 · Glass

PULSE
PULSE

Specular highlight, translucent body, a bright rim. Reads as a lens — "looking into" the data. Best on dark; on light it can wash out.

3 · Dark core rec

PULSE
PULSE

Navy sphere with a bright cyan centre and a soft outer glow. Serious, weighty, and the glow makes it the brightest thing on the page without being loud. Holds up on both backgrounds. The thinking state is the core brightening — no shape change needed.

4 · Aurora

PULSE
PULSE

A slowly turning blue-teal-violet ring around a pale centre. Distinctly "AI" in the current visual language. Risk: it's also the most generic — several products use this exact motif.

5 · Halo

PULSE
PULSE

The radial orb with two faint concentric halos on the page behind it. Gives it presence without size. The halos can breathe with the heartbeat later — this is the look that carries the "pulse" motion best.

6 · Ringed

PULSE
PULSE

Orb plus a thin broken ring that turns very slowly. Planetary. The ring speeds up when thinking, so the state change is free. Risk: two elements at 44px can look fussy.

7 · Horizon

PULSE
PULSE

Hard split — pale sky above, deep sea below. Graphic rather than glossy; closest to a logo. Sits well beside flat UI. Risk: the split line can read as a toggle switch.

8 · Emissive

PULSE
PULSE

White-hot centre, wide soft bloom. It emits. Spectacular on dark, and it's the one that most looks like it's alive. On light it needs a slightly darker body or the bloom disappears.

9 · Halo × Emissive your idea

PULSE  ·  idle · idle · thinking
PULSE  ·  idle · idle · thinking

The white-hot core and bloom of 8, sitting inside the three concentric halos of 5. The halos breathe on the same 5.5s cycle as the body, so the whole thing pulses outward — which is the name. Thinking is the same animation at 1.1s: the bloom widens, the halos push out faster. No shape change, no spinner. On light the body is a shade deeper than 8 so the bloom still reads; on dark it is the brightest object on screen by a wide margin. This is better than either parent — 8 was dark-only and 5 was quiet; together the glow has somewhere to go.

spec · body 44px (56 on the empty state) · core #fff→#c9ecff→#48b4ee→#186592→#0b3552 · bloom 14px/3px → 22px/6px on the beat · halos at +7 / +15 / +24px, alpha .13 / .07 / .035 · cycle 5.5s idle, 1.1s thinking · badge sits on the outer halo edge

My pick was 3 — until 9. 3 has real weight, it's the brightest point on any page without shouting, it works on both light and dark HealthFlow themes, and its thinking state is simply the core getting brighter. 9 takes what 8 does on dark and makes it work on light too, and it is the only one whose idle motion is a pulse. I'd ship 9.
