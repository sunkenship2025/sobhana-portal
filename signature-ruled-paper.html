What the line detector found
How the rules are removed. A vote over near-horizontal and near-vertical slopes (a Hough restricted to +/-8 degrees, which is why the tilt does not matter): for every slope and intercept, count ink pixels on that line. Anything polling above vote x width is a printed rule, not handwriting. Deleting it is the careful part — where the signature crosses a rule, the ink run perpendicular to the line is thick, so only runs thinner than the limit are cleared. That is what keeps the flourish intact through every crossing instead of leaving it chopped into segments.
