Ad behaviour — photos & video

  • Formats: JPG / PNG / WebP images, MP4 / WebM video
  • Video sound: muted by default (waiting-room friendly); optional per-item
  • Duration: photos hold a set time; videos play their full length (with a max cap)
  • Fit: per-creative — fill the frame, or letterbox on white
  • Interrupt: a call pauses video / freezes photo, shows Now Serving, then resumes or advances
  • Schedule & weight: date range, time-of-day, branch, rotation frequency per creative
