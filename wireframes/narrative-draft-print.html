Print a narrative report before it is finalized

For doctors who read better on paper. The printout is the same page the patient would get, so line and page breaks can be checked, but it is clearly marked as a draft, has no signature, and records who printed it and when. It prints whatever is on screen: if cloud sync is off, the button saves first.

E — Ctrl + P while typing a report free add-on to A

Today Ctrl+P prints the app screen — sidebar, toolbar, half a report. With the cursor in a narrative, Ctrl+P would do exactly what “Print draft” does. Costs a few lines; catches the doctor who prints out of habit.

3 · The words on the stamp

Band headlineReads as
DRAFT — NOT A CERTIFIED REPORTPlain, staff and patients both understand it; “certified” answers exactly the worry (is this valid?)recommended
REVIEW COPY — NOT FOR PATIENTSays who it's for, but doesn't say it's invalid if it leaksok
UNSIGNED DRAFT — NOT VALID FOR DIAGNOSISStrongest legally; long, and “diagnosis” sounds alarming if a patient sees itok
PROOF COPY / DUMMY REPORTPrinting-press / testing words — doctors and patients don't use themno

4 · Defaults I'll use unless you say otherwise

Paper: letterhead layout, same as your normal PrintThe draft prints in the pre-printed-letterhead layout (no logo drawn), exactly like the final printout, so page breaks on the draft are the page breaks the patient gets. If plain paper is in the tray it still reads fine; if letterhead is in the tray, the band + watermark still mark it.
Signature: never on a draftName and designation stay so the doctor can check them; the signature image and stamp are replaced by the dashed “Not signed — draft” box.
Who can print: anyone who can type the reportNot limited to owner / lab in-charge — the doctors who need it usually can't finalize.
Empty report: button disabledNothing typed yet → nothing to print.
