Pay-Run · what the partner concept changes

Outside Labs is configuration. Rates, arrangements, doors. Nothing settles there. Pay-Run is the money — and it now carries a payee that can owe in either direction, which today's header cannot say.

The bug, as shipped
COMMISSION PAYOUTS
₹2,06,989.02
What you owe referrers & clinics
OUTSIDE-LAB PAYABLES
₹-379.66
What you owe outside labs for outsourced tests
A negative payable under “what you owe”. We owe Lalitha nothing — she owes us ₹379.66. The number is right; the frame is wrong. A two-sided payee cannot live in a one-directional KPI.
Option A — three headline numbers, signed by meaning recommended
COMMISSION PAYOUTS
₹2,06,989.02
Referrers & consulting doctors
WE OWE PARTNERS
₹0
Vendor rates & their cut on work we billed
PARTNERS OWE US
₹379.66
Our share on work they billed
Referral Doctors ₹1,11,289.02 (60) · Consulting ₹95,700 (6) · Partners ₹379.66 in (1)

Why. Never render money you are owed as a negative of money you owe. Two tiles, two colours, no minus signs — the caution tone Pay-Run already uses for payables, the healthy tone for receivables. A partner who is both directions shows in both tiles and nets on its own row.

Option B — keep two tiles, flip the one label
PARTNERS — THEY OWE US
₹379.66
Flips to “We owe partners” when the net goes the other way

Smallest change. But a tile whose meaning flips month to month is a tile you misread once and trust never again.
