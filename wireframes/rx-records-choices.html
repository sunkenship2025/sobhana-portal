Prescription records: design choices

Every option uses the same six visits so you can compare them. The six cover every state: signed and sent · revised and printed · draft · No Rx given (the doctor closed it without one) · Paper (reception closed it) · signed but the online link is off.

1 · Finalized OP / IP rows

Where reception finds a finished visit and hands over the prescription and the bill.

AIcon buttons: today's row with a Send button addedclosest to what staff know

The status chip sits next to Completed. 📄 opens the prescription sheet (preview + Print). 💬 sends it and turns green once sent. 👁 is the details dialog, which gets one status line.

GoodAlmost nothing changes for staff. Narrow rows, works on a phone.
WeakIcons need tooltips to be understood. "Sent" is only a green tint.

CDocument tiles: each document is a small card

No chip. The prescription and the bill each get a tile showing their own state, and tapping a tile opens that document. Send and Print are inside the opened sheet.

GoodStatus and action in one place, so you see "signed, sent, read" at a glance. Scales if a third document (reports) joins later.
WeakTallest rows, fewer visits per screen. Sending takes one extra tap (open, then Send).

DExpand in place: the row opens like the Patient 360 panel

The row stays short, with just the chip. Tapping it expands a Prescription line and a Bill line, with the same buttons and "Printed / Sent" lines as the Patient 360 panel. No dialog.

GoodSame layout as Patient 360, so staff learn it once. Full status only when you want it.
WeakEvery action takes a tap to expand first. The busiest counter action (send) is one level down.

2 · Doctor with no signature yet

The signature is managed in the doctor's profile, once. The doctor's screens only point there.

N1Note on the sign bar only
You haven't added your signature yet, so this can't be signed.Add it in My profile →

Shown only at review time, where it matters. The draft is saved first, so nothing is lost on the way to the profile and back.

N2Also a line on the queuerecommended
Add your signature in My profile so your prescriptions can be signed.Open My profile →

One line at the top of the doctor's queue until a signature exists, so they find out before the first patient rather than mid-consultation. The sign bar gets N1's note as well.

3 · The doctor's profile: everything about them in one place

Details, signature, letterhead note and password, all on one page. The details print on a legal document, so the only open question is who may change them.
